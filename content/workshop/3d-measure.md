## 3D Measurement & PMI

Forma is a Godot-based prototype for inspecting 3D models, taking measurements, and working with PMI annotations.

### Features

- Measure distances, straight-edge lengths, and angles by selecting points, edges, and planes.
- Create and edit datums, datum targets, geometric tolerances, surface roughness symbols, and text annotations.
- Import GLB/glTF models and experiment with STEP AP242 and PMI workflows.
- Reposition annotations and save or load them as JSON.

![STEP PMI viewed from another angle in Forma](/images/3d-measure-annotations.png)

### Controls

- **Right-drag** to orbit; **middle-drag** or **Shift + right-drag** to pan.
- **Scroll** to zoom; **F** to fit the model; **R** to reset the view.
- **Left-click** to select geometry and place measurements.
- **Esc** to cancel.
